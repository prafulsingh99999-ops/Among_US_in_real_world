CREWMATE PROTOCOL

Win Condition: Complete All Tasks OR Eject All Impostors
  • PHYSICAL RFID TASK CHECKPOINTS: Carry your SPARK Clearance Badge. Scan at designated Arduino stations across IIIT Bhopal campus to validate tasks.
  • DEAD BODY REPORTING: If you find a eliminated crewmate with their red marker placed down, loudly shout "DEAD BODY!" and notify a SPARK Proctor or hit the nearest buzzer.
  • GHOST SILENCE PROTOCOL: Eliminated players become Ghosts. You may NOT speak, gesture, or assist living crewmates, but must complete your remaining tasks to help the win bar!
  • NO PHYSICAL VIOLENCE: All eliminations are contactless via assigned RFID / NFC tags and sticker tap mechanics.

IMPOSTOR PROTOCOL

Win Condition: Match Crewmate Headcount OR Reactor Sabotage
  • STEALTH ELIMINATION: Tag a crewmate on the shoulder when isolated without witnesses. Hand them the "ELIMINATED" marker card.
  • SABOTAGE PROTOCOLS: Impostors can trigger automated campus emergency alarms (Lights Off, Oxygen Depletion, Reactor Meltdown) via the SPARK mobile terminal.
  • KILL COOLDOWN: Impostor digital scanners enforce a 45-second mandatory cooldown timer between eliminations.
  • VENT ROAMING: Designated secret pathways and stairwells marked as "VENTS" are accessible ONLY to confirmed Impostor passes!
