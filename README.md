## ✨ Features & Highlights

### 🚨 1. Interactive Opening Cutscene: Emergency Buzzer & Meeting Table
* **Step 1 (Open Protective Case)**: The console loads with an authentic Among Us Emergency Buzzer covered by a 3D glass protective lid. Click the lid to flip it open upwards with a realistic mechanical click and hinge sound!
* **Step 2 (Press Emergency Button)**: Press the exposed glowing red button to sound the alarm!
* **Step 3 (Red Alert Calling Screen)**: The entire background shifts to a dramatic **flashing RED siren alert** with rotating emergency beacons and the official sound effect: **`Among Us (Emergency Meeting) - Sound Effect (HD).mp3`**.
* **Step 4 (The Skeld Meeting Table)**: All 8 crewmates gather around the glowing meeting table.
  * Crewmates panic and ask: *"Why???", "Who died?!", "Where???"*
  * **Red Crewmate answers**: *"SPARK club is organizing THE GREAT REAL LIFE AMONG US TOURNAMENT at IIIT Bhopal!"*
  * Red details: **Venue (IIIT Bhopal Campus)**, **Date (9th Nov 2026)**, **Time (7:00 PM Onwards)**, **100% Free Registration**, and **Exciting Prizes for Winners!**
  * Crewmates celebrate: *"REAL LIFE AMONG US?! LETS GOOO!", "Free registration?! Sign me up!"*
* **Step 5 (Meeting Over -> Website Opens)**: The meeting is adjourned and the main website smoothly unfolds.
* **Replay & Skip**: Includes a top-right **Skip Intro** option, plus a **🎬 Replay Intro** button in the navbar to re-watch the cutscene anytime!

### 🌌 2. Live Interactive Space Background
* **3-Layer Parallax Starfield**: Over 280 twinkling stars that respond smoothly to mouse cursor and gyroscope movements.
* **Shooting Stars / Meteors**: Dynamic shooting stars with glowing light trails streaking across the cosmic void.
* **Nebula Dust Clouds**: Soft ambient radial cosmic dust in crimson, cyan, and deep violet.
* **Deep Space Drifting Crewmates**: Miniature astronauts tumbling slowly in the background.
* **Hyperspace Warp Acceleration**: Speed-warp effect triggered during Emergency Meetings!

### 🧑‍🚀 2. Crewmate Customizer & Character Lab
* **High-Res Scalable Vector SVGs**: Pixel-accurate Among Us characters featuring backpacks, visors, highlights, and custom shadows.
* **12 Canonical Suit Colors**: Red, Blue, Green, Pink, Orange, Yellow, Black, White, Purple, Cyan, Lime, and Brown.
* **Equippable Headgear**: Plant Sprout, DUM Sticky Note, Mini Crewmate, Top Hat, Royal Crown, Party Cone, and Viking Horns.
* **Secret Role Card**: Click to test your clearance and reveal if you are a **Crewmate** or the **Impostor** (with suspense horror audio sting).
* **Full-Screen Ejection Sequence**: Watch your custom crewmate tumble across the stars with authentic typewriter text: *"Red was not An Impostor. 1 Impostor remains."*
* **Continuous Walking Marquee**: Animated crewmates walking across the screen.

### 🚨 3. Emergency Meeting System
* **Big Pulsing Emergency Buzzer**: Centrally located siren button with caution stripes and alarm glow.
* **Procedural Klaxon Siren**: Dual-tone emergency alarm synthesized via Web Audio API.
* **Red Alert Visual Override**: Strobe warning lights, yellow caution borders, and incident briefing.

### 🎮 4. Playable Skeld Task Terminal (Mini-Games)
* **Fix Wiring (Electrical)**: Connect matching color terminals (Red, Blue, Yellow, Magenta) with live electrical spark audio.
* **Swipe Card (Admin)**: Realistic card swipe slot with speed detection (*"Too Fast"*, *"Too Slow"*, *"Card Accepted"*).
* **Unlock Manifolds (Reactor)**: Click numeric sequence 1 to 10 in order; mistakes reset the reactor pressure.
* **MedBay Biometric Scan**: Holographic vertical laser scan measuring crewmate weight, blood type, and impostor risk index.

### 🗺️ 5. Campus Skeld Radar Map
* Interactive blueprint of **IIIT Bhopal Campus** mapped to Skeld rooms:
  * ⚡ **Electrical**: Robotics & IoT Lab (Arduino relays & wire circuits)
  * ☕ **Cafeteria**: College Canteen / Lawn (Emergency Buzzer & Chute)
  * 💉 **MedBay**: Student Health Wing (Biometric Scanner)
  * 💳 **Admin**: Academic Admin Wing (RFID swipe reader & logs)
  * 📹 **Security**: Server Control Room (CCTV hallway monitors)
  * ⚛️ **Reactor**: AI / HPC Cluster (Meltdown handplates)

### 🪪 6. Mission Registration & Boarding Pass Generator
* Cadets register with Name, Roll Number, Branch, and Contact.
* Generates a sleek, holographic **SPARK Security Clearance ID Badge** complete with barcode, QR code, and chosen Crewmate avatar.
* Includes **Print Badge** button with dedicated print stylesheet.

### 🔊 7. Zero-Dependency Web Audio API Synthesizer
* 100% self-contained audio generation (no missing MP3 files or network latency).
* Features: Emergency Siren, Crewmate Squeak/Step, Kill Suspense Sting, Task Complete Arpeggio, Electrical Zaps, Card Swipe Beeps, and the iconic "SHH!" whisper.
* Includes bottom audio control toggle.
